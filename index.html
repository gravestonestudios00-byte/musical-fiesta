A large Chrome OS friendly browser adventure

Chrono Grove:
Festival of Sparks

Restore eight floating groves, survive multi-stage encounters, unlock relics, and finish the moonlit festival campaign. The campaign is paced for roughly 40–55 minutes of play.

Move: WASD / arrowsAim: mouse / touchpadDash: SpaceInteract: E

How to play

  • Collect sparks and return them to the festival beacon.
  • Defeat sentinels to earn gear, tempo, and relic choices.
  • Each grove has patrols, a mini-event, and a boss wave before the next island unlocks.
  • Chromebook tip: zoom the browser to 100% and use fullscreen for best performance.
